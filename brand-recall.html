Month-by-month brand recall from GA4, using the Digital Brand Recall Ratio. Expanded to a full year so we can separate seasonality from a real mix shift.

Source: GA4 Data API · property 341895442 · Sep 1, 2025–Sep 23, 2026. Direct on Jul 27–28 and Aug 4–5, 2026 replaced with the same 2025 dates (bot attack).

Digital Brand Recall Ratio (%) = (Direct sessions + Branded organic search sessions) ÷ Total website sessions
Seasonality is real — it does not explain the recent drop

Volume is seasonal (December is the quiet month both years; late spring is stronger). The recall ratio also has a mild seasonal wiggle: June is a local high, July usually dips. That July dip happened in 2025 too (44.2%). Last year it recovered in August to 47.1%. This year it did not — August is 40.4% and September is 39.2% vs 44.8% a year ago. The February 2026 plunge to 31.7% is not winter seasonality; paid Cross-network, Display, and Paid Search spiked and diluted the mix.

June peak is seasonal

June was the high in both years (49.3% in 2025, 50.8% in 2026). Treating May–June as a brand-strong window is fair.

July dip is seasonal; the hangover is not

July fell both years. 2025 bounced back in August. 2026 is still 6.7 points below last August and 5.6 below last September.

Feb–Mar 2026 is paid dilution

Sessions jumped to ~16.7k / 16.6k vs ~11.4k / 12.6k last year. New Cross-network, Display, and Paid Search — not a winter brand slump. Direct actually rose.

Definitions

Brand-page organic: Organic Search landing on homepage, about, contact, membership, patient portal, locations, clinic welcome pages, or clinician bios. Homepage-only: Organic Search landing on / only. Google hides almost all organic queries in GA4, so these are landing-page proxies. Calendar 2025 averaged 46.6%; 2026 through Sep 23 is 42.1%.
