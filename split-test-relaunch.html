Link CTR and CPC

CTR is link clicks ÷ impressions. CPC is spend ÷ link clicks. Through this morning, Image 4 leads link CTR at 0.868%. Image 5 is next at 0.808%. Image 6 is at 0.762% and is the cheapest click at $0.28. That order is this morning’s cheap inventory. Through the completed Oct 21, Image 6 led link CTR at 0.947%, Image 4 was at 0.931%, and Image 5 was at 0.826%. Oct 22 is still a partial morning.
