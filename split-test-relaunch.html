Link CTR and CPC

CTR is link clicks ÷ impressions. CPC is spend ÷ link clicks. Through Oct 19, Image 6 leads link CTR at 1.006% and is also the cheapest click at $0.33. Image 4 is close on price at $0.34. Image 5 trails on both. Oct 19 is still a partial day.

Blended CTR has separated

Image 6 is at 1.006%, Image 4 at 0.888%, and Image 5 at 0.785%. Those gaps are outside sampling noise (Image 6 vs Image 4 p = 0.002, Image 6 vs Image 5 p < 0.001, Image 4 vs Image 5 p = 0.006).

Image 6 wins CPC

$0.33 per link click vs $0.34 (Image 4) and $0.45 (Image 5).

Oct 19 is partial

So far today Image 5 spent $36.90 and received 39,072 impressions at a $0.94 CPM, so its link CTR fell to 0.48% and its CPC fell to $0.20. Image 4 was 2.47% and $0.36. Image 6 was 2.05% and $0.40. From Oct 15 on, Image 6’s link CTR pulled ahead on the tighter-inventory days: 3.41% on Oct 16 and 5.31% on Oct 17. On Oct 18 Image 6 again found cheap inventory and its CTR fell to 0.63%. Oct 19 is still partial.

Daily CPC

Image 4’s CPC fell to $0.18 with the cheap Oct 10 inventory. During the Oct 13 evening restart, all three CPCs fell to about $0.10. On Oct 17 Image 6’s CPC was $0.25 on 209 link clicks. On Oct 18 it was $0.25 again, with 26,631 impressions at a $1.59 CPM. On the partial Oct 19, Image 5’s CPC fell to $0.20 with the cheap inventory.

Y-axis: USD per link click. Gaps are days with no delivery.

Daily impressions

Each ad set still has a $30 daily budget. Meta bills for impressions and bids lowest cost for a link click, so those dollars buy as many impressions as the auction will sell. On Oct 10, Image 4 spent $22.96 and received 34,868 impressions at a $0.66 CPM. Image 5 spent $25.88 and received 3,650 impressions at a $7.09 CPM. Image 4 did not receive extra budget. Its inventory was cheaper, which is also why its link CTR fell to 0.37% and its CPC fell to $0.18 that day.

Oct 11–12 had no delivery. Oct 13 ran from 10 PM to midnight ET: each ad spent about $8 and received 22,461–27,187 impressions at a $0.30–$0.38 CPM. Oct 14 spend ran to $47.96, $52.03, and $51.89. On Oct 18, Image 6 spent $42.27 and received 26,631 impressions at a $1.59 CPM, while Image 5 spent $30.63 and received 3,051 impressions at a $10.04 CPM. Oct 19, still partial, flipped that pattern: Image 5 spent $36.90 and received 39,072 impressions at a $0.94 CPM, while Image 4 and Image 6 had spent $15.25 and $12.34. Meta can spend above a daily budget on a single day. The ad set budgets are still $30.
