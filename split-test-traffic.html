Link CTR and CPC

CTR is link clicks ÷ impressions. CPC is spend ÷ link clicks. V1 is the click-rate winner. V3 is the cheapest click. V2 lost both.

Y-axis: link CTR (%).

Y-axis: CPC (USD per link click).

V1 wins CTR

0.613% link CTR vs 0.435% (V3) and 0.309% (V2). The CTR gaps are significant (p < 0.001 vs V2, p = 0.000015 vs V3).

V3 wins CPC

$0.38 per link click vs $0.43 (V1) and $0.47 (V2). Same budget, more clicks, cheaper clicks.

V2 is the weak headline

Feel Freaking Amazing had the most impressions (cheap inventory on Sep 29) and the worst CTR. The line did not earn the extra delivery.
