# Five Journeys Marketing — end of 2027

## At The End of 2027

Marketing sets the tone for a concierge experience from initial awareness to final sign-up and beyond. Lead generation, prospect nurturing, and customer engagement inspire other teams to deliver exceptional service, helping them champion the Five Journeys value at every stage of the member lifecycle.

We closed the year with a **40% year-over-year increase in net new patients** compared to 2026. This net growth includes all membership levels across Newton, Boston, and future locations (calculated as new sign-ups minus reconciled departures). This includes a major shift in our tier mix: new sign-ups moved from majority Standard to over 80% Silver, and Gold memberships grew from 128 to over 200.

The average **Net Promoter Score (NPS)** consistently trends **above 9**, and we receive **3 to 5 Google 5-star reviews every week**. We will have implemented a programmatic solution for generating consistent reviews, made the NPS process effortless, and acted upon results in real time to adapt and improve our services.

Our team operates on an **AI-forward, automated infrastructure**. The entire marketing lifecycle—from asset creation and deployment to testing and real-time optimization—runs autonomously, allowing us to focus on continuous strategy improvement and campaign performance.

The Five Journeys website, alongside its supplement store, has emerged as a premier hub of credible, accurate information—making it an invaluable, highly visible resource for individuals leveraging AI and LLMs to research their health.

As a result, our operations are **mature and ready to scale**. The systems we built require no restructuring to support expansion into new markets and states.

## Marketing SOW

Marketing Departments work spans three interrelated areas:

1. Member acquisition
2. Member retention
3. Special projects and initiatives

Marketing operations encompass a spectrum of initiatives from research and planning to creative execution and evaluation. To support this work, the team deploys campaigns and assets across 14 distinct channels utilizing a stack of 24 specialized tools and outlets.

The work answers “what does it take?”

1. Research identifies the ideal client and what they need or want.
2. Planning develops an experience better than competitors.
3. Creation creates compelling, brand-aligned assets and campaigns.
4. Execution makes the experience meet or exceed the expectation.
5. Evaluation gathers the feedback and adapts, and goes back to creation and execution.
