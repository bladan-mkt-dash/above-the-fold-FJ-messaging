Words used on this page
  • Indexable means Google is allowed to list the page.
  • Sitemap means the address list the site gives Google.
  • Cache means a saved copy so the next visit is faster. It is on again as of 26 Oct 2026 (HIT confirmed).
  • Main content time (LCP) means how long the largest visible piece takes to appear. Google’s good line is 2.5 seconds.
  • Field data means speed scores from real visitors. Those are missing.
  • Lab score means a simulated phone test (Lighthouse). It is not field data.
  • Main heading (H1) means the page’s top heading. There should be one.
  • Structured data (JSON-LD) means the hidden summary Google may use for extra search lines.
  • Noindex means a tag that asks Google not to list that page.
